How Does PHYX Work?

Generic AI tutors only grade final answers and forget what a student struggles with between turns. PHYX maintains a persistent probabilistic learner model over the 2026–27 NCERT Class 10 Physics prerequisite graph—diagnosing why a mistake happened and adapting difficulty and spaced reviews automatically.

01.Diagnostic Placement

A 5-question cross-syllabus assessment probes Light, Human Eye, Electricity, and Magnetism simultaneously to initialize mastery priors and catch early sign-convention or circuit misconceptions.

02.Prerequisite Concept DAG

All 13 NCERT Class 10 Physics concepts (P1–P13) are connected in a Directed Acyclic Graph. Mastery updates propagate along prerequisite edges so foundational gaps are strengthened first.

03.Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT)

After every answer, mastery probability P(L) updates via Bayes' rule using difficulty-calibrated Slip and Guess parameters—preventing single lucky guesses from inflating mastery scores.

04.Reasoning Misconception Detection

Students submit step-by-step reasoning alongside MCQ or subjective answers. Even if an MCQ option is guessed right, flawed or missing reasoning triggers conservative BKT updates and targeted remediation.

05.SM-2 Spaced Repetition

Each concept tracks an Easiness Factor (EF) and review interval. Missed concepts reschedule in 12 hours, while verified concepts expand intervals (1d → 3d → 7d+).

06.Prof. Aria Lectures & 8 Visual Labs

Students can pause Prof. Aria's step-by-step derivations mid-sentence to ask live doubts, manipulate 8 real-time physics simulations, and use hands-free voice-to-text input.
